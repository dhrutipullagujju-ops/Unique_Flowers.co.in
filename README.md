# 🌸 Unique Flowers

### Little blooms, handmade with love.

A cheerful storefront for **Unique Flowers**, where pipe cleaners become colourful bouquets, thoughtful gifts, and DIY flower kits.

## ✨ Find your kind of flower

Browse six collections, each with its own styles to explore:

- 🌷 Single Flowers
- 💐 Mini Bouquets
- 🌼 Medium Bouquets
- 🌻 Premium Bouquets
- 🎀 Customised Bouquets
- 🧵 DIY Kits

## 💝 Made personal

Create a custom bouquet by choosing its size, flower varieties, colours, and an optional gift-tag message. Add it to your bag alongside other favourites, adjust quantities, and send your order through WhatsApp for confirmation.

## 🚀 Open the shop

No setup or installation needed: open `index.html` in a browser. Google Fonts need an internet connection; fallback fonts are included.

## 🧰 Under the petals

- `catalog.js` — collection and product details

- `bouquets.js` — collection directory
- `collection-page.js` — collection pages and custom bouquet builder
- `bag.js` — bag quantities and WhatsApp checkout
